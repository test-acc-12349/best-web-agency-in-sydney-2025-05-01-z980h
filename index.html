Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical project timeline?

Most projects are completed within 4-8 weeks, depending on complexity. We follow a structured development process: 1 week for planning, 2-4 weeks for design and development, and 1-2 weeks for testing and refinement.

What maintenance and support do you provide?

We offer comprehensive maintenance packages including 24/7 monitoring, regular updates, security patches, and technical support. Our team is available via phone, email, and chat with guaranteed response times.

How do you handle website security?

Security is our top priority. We implement SSL certificates, regular security audits, automated backups, and advanced firewall protection. All our sites include malware scanning and DDoS protection.

What is your pricing structure?

We provide customized quotes based on project requirements. Basic websites start at $5,000, while e-commerce solutions typically range from $10,000-$25,000. All projects include hosting, maintenance, and support packages.

Do you provide SEO services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive SEO services including keyword research, on-page optimization, technical SEO, and content strategy. All our websites are built with SEO best practices in mind from the ground up.
